Our story

Built on relationships that already exist

RIG was founded on a simple observation: world-class medical institutions and ambitious companies are constantly looking for each other — but language, distance and unfamiliar business practice keep them apart.

We started with the relationships we already trusted: clinicians in Japan, partners in Malaysia and Southeast Asia, and corporations on both sides looking for a credible way in. Regenesis International Group formalized that network — and the quiet, personal way it works.

What guides us

Three principles

Networks over noise

An introduction only where it creates real value. We would rather make ten careful introductions than a hundred careless ones.

Cross-border by design

Japan–Southeast Asia is our home ground. Language, culture, scheduling, expectations — we navigate both sides natively.

Long-term trust

We work quietly and precisely, in service of our partners' goals. We measure success in partnerships that last.

What we are — and are not

Clarity about our role

Regenesis International Group is a business development and liaison company. Being precise about what that means — and what it does not — is part of the trust we ask our partners to place in us.

What we are

  • Business development and liaison professionals
  • Introducers and coordinators between institutions and companies
  • Cross-border communication and scheduling partners
  • Cultural and commercial interpreters on both sides

What we are not

  • A hospital, clinic, or medical practice of any kind
  • A provider of medical treatment or medical advice
  • A party to clinical decisions — those remain with licensed professionals
  • A regulator, guarantor, or cell-processing facility
