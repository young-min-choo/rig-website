Important notices

Disclaimer & scope.

Last updated October 5, 2026 · Applies to regenesisinternationalgroup.com and all RIG materials.

1. About this notice

Regenesis International Group ("RIG") publishes this notice to define clearly the scope of its services and to explain what RIG is — and is not. This notice applies to this website and to all introductions, coordination and services provided by RIG.

Clarity at the start is part of the trust we build on. If anything below is unclear, please contact us before engaging with our services.

2. What RIG is

RIG is an international business development and liaison company. Our services are administrative and commercial in nature: discovery, matchmaking, introductions, coordination and relationship management between medical institutions, corporations, research partners and service providers.

We operate primarily across Japan, Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

3. What RIG is not

RIG is not a hospital, clinic, medical practice, pharmacy, laboratory, or cell-processing facility. RIG does not employ licensed medical professionals for the purpose of providing care, and does not act as a healthcare provider in any capacity.

RIG does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medication, perform medical procedures, determine treatment eligibility, or deliver any clinical service.

4. No medical advice

Nothing on this website constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a recommendation for any individual case. Content describing regenerative medicine, stem cells, institutions or therapies is general information about a field and an ecosystem — not guidance for any person's medical situation.

Medical decisions belong exclusively to you and the licensed professionals at the institutions involved. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider regarding any medical condition or treatment.

5. Independent institutions

Hospitals, clinics, physicians, laboratories, and other providers mentioned, introduced, or engaged through RIG operate independently. RIG is not an agent, employer, or legal representative of any such institution, and introduction through RIG does not create a treatment relationship with RIG.

Each institution's own professionals make all clinical determinations, and each institution's own terms, consent processes and applicable regulations govern any care or service it provides.

6. No outcomes promised

RIG does not promise, warrant, or guarantee any medical outcome, treatment success, improvement, cure, or result of any kind. Any outcomes discussed relate to the institutions and partners we introduce — never to services RIG itself provides.

Where treatment is involved, its safety and efficacy are matters for the medical institutions providing it under their applicable regulatory frameworks.

7. Regulatory context

Regenerative medicine in Japan operates under national law (including the Act on the Safety of Regenerative Medicine) and comparable frameworks in other jurisdictions, with institutions subject to review and notification requirements. RIG does not hold, provide, or replace any such regulatory status; where a partner institution describes its own approvals, that institution's disclosures govern.

8. No professional advisory services

RIG's services do not constitute legal, regulatory, financial, investment, or tax advice. Opportunities, market observations, and any commercial matters discussed in the course of liaison are general in nature. Engage your own qualified advisors for such matters.

9. Third-party information

Information about institutions, partners, or markets on this website or shared during engagements is provided in good faith. While RIG takes care in what it communicates, it makes no warranty as to the completeness or accuracy of third-party information and is not liable for reliance on it.

10. Contact

Questions about this notice or the scope of our services may be directed to contact@regenesisinternationalgroup.com.
