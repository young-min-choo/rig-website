About RIG

A trusted bridge in the regenerative medicine ecosystem

Regenesis International Group is an international business development and liaison company. We connect medical institutions with corporations, research partners and service providers so that the right parties find each other — across languages, borders and regulatory environments.

Our focus is regenerative medicine: the hospitals, clinics, researchers and companies shaping the next generation of healthcare. We work quietly, precisely, and always in service of our partners' goals.

  • Networks over noise. Introductions only where they create real value.
  • Cross-border by design. Japan–Southeast Asia is our home ground.
  • Long-term trust. We measure success in partnerships that last.
