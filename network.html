Our network

Two hubs, one corridor.

Tokyo and Kuala Lumpur anchor our relationships across Japan, Malaysia, Southeast Asia — and partner institutions worldwide.

Hub

Tokyo, Japan

Our Japan base sits at the center of one of the world's most advanced healthcare systems — and at the front door of regenerative medicine's fastest-moving research and clinical institutions.

From here we work with hospitals, clinics and research teams, and coordinate the Japan-side expectations, scheduling and business practice that make international partnerships actually work.

Hub

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Malaysia anchors our Southeast Asia presence — a market with rising investment in healthcare, biotechnology and medical tourism, and a natural bridge between Japan and the wider region.

From Kuala Lumpur we scout opportunities, maintain partner relationships and manage the Southeast Asia side of every engagement with local fluency.
