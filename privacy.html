Privacy Policy

Interim notice. Our full Privacy Policy is being finalised and will be published here before this site accepts online enquiries. The facts below describe how this website works today.

What this website collects

Browsing this site collects nothing about you: there are no accounts, cookies, trackers, analytics or advertising requests, and fonts are served from this site itself.

If you send an enquiry, we receive only what you enter in the form:

  • Private enquiry: name, email, WhatsApp number (optional) and your message.
  • Partner enquiry: name, position, organisation, country, email and your message.

How it is used

Enquiry details are used solely to respond to you. They are not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Please do not send medical records or health details through the website. Where an enquiry proceeds, your coordinator will arrange a secure channel and explain how your information is shared with the partner clinic, with your consent.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete information you have sent us, write to info@regenig.com.
