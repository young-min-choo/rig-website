What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and the enquiries you send through it. If your enquiry moves on to a consultation, your coordinator will give you a separate notice and ask for your consent before any health information is collected or shared.

What we collect

When you browse. We use no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we load nothing from third-party sites. Our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address so it can deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. We do not use that data to identify you.

When you send an enquiry, we receive only what you enter:

  • Private enquiry: name, email, WhatsApp number (optional) and your message.
  • Partner enquiry: name, position, organisation, country, email and your message.

Health information

Please do not send medical records or health details through this website. If an enquiry includes them anyway, we use them only to reply to you, and [PROPOSAL] we remove them from the enquiry record once we have replied.

Health information is sensitive personal data. We collect it only later in the process, through a secure channel, with your explicit consent, and only for arranging your introduction to a partner clinic.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange any follow-up you ask for (based on your consent).
  • To introduce you to a partner clinic or institution, only once you agree to it.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host this website, deliver form submissions and run our email. They act only on our instructions and under contract.
  • Partner clinics and institutions in Japan, only with your explicit consent, and only what they need to assess your enquiry.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

International transfers

RIG is based in Malaysia, our partner clinics are in Japan, and our service providers may process data in other countries. We transfer personal data abroad only with your consent or where the law permits it, and we require recipients to protect it to a standard comparable to Malaysian law.

How long we keep it

[PROPOSAL] Private enquiries that do not proceed are deleted within 12 months of our last contact. Partner enquiries are kept for up to 24 months. Where an enquiry proceeds, the retention periods in your separate consultation notice apply.

Security

We limit access to staff who need it, and we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and providers with recognised security practices. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • stop using it or withdraw your consent;
  • delete it, where we are not required to keep it;
  • send it to you or another organisation in a portable format.

Write to info@regenig.com. We reply within 21 days. You may also complain to Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Commissioner, or, where Japanese law applies, to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission.
