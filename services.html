What we do

Four disciplines, one bridge.

Business development and liaison services for the regenerative medicine ecosystem — from first introduction to working partnership.

01 · Institution Networking

The right counterpart, already vetted

We connect hospitals and clinics with vetted partners — specialist providers, suppliers, research institutions — matched to each partner's actual needs rather than a directory search.

Every introduction is made with context: who you are meeting, why them, and what a good outcome looks like for both sides.

You get
  • Vetted institution & partner introductions
  • Needs-matched counterpart selection
  • Context briefing before every meeting

02 · Regenerative Medicine Liaison

Fluency in the stem cell ecosystem

Coordination across the stem cell and regenerative medicine ecosystem: institutions, suppliers and corporations, introduced with cultural and commercial fluency.

From therapy providers to research teams to corporate alliances, we understand both the science-adjacent vocabulary and the business practice — on both sides of the corridor.

You get
  • Ecosystem-wide institutional introductions
  • Science-literate, commercially fluent coordination
  • Discretion throughout

03 · Corporate Partnerships

Entering healthcare with a trustworthy path

For corporations entering the healthcare and biotechnology space internationally — opportunity scouting, partner introductions and relationship management.

a

Scouting

We map the landscape on the ground: institutions, gaps, and realistic opportunities that match your strategy.

b

Introductions

Vetted meetings with the institutions and partners who matter — with context, not cold outreach.

c

Relationship management

After the handshake, we stay in the loop — coordination, follow-through, and long-term stewardship.

04 · Cross-Border Operations

The practical layer that makes it work

Practical Japan–Southeast Asia coordination: communication, scheduling, and navigation of local business practice on both sides.

Communication

Bilingual coordination and translation-adjacent support, so nothing is lost between Japanese and English/Malay business communication.

Scheduling

Time zones, calendars, travel windows — handled on both sides so meetings actually happen.

Business practice

Local etiquette, expectations and pace — navigated natively on the Japan side and the Southeast Asia side.

Scope

What our engagements are — and are not

Every engagement is business development and liaison work. We stay precisely inside that boundary.

Engagements include

  • Institution & corporate introductions
  • Partnership coordination and follow-through
  • Cross-border scheduling and communication
  • Market-entry scouting support

Engagements never include

  • Medical treatment, advice, or clinical services
  • Clinical decision-making of any kind
  • Regulatory approvals or certifications
  • Anything outside business development and liaison
